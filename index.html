The case

What you'll do, in five short sprints.

A legacy export of 1,300+ Tatort episodes has been handled by too many people: duplicate records, invisible characters, broadcasters hiding behind aliases, and dates in four different disguises. Working like a forensic team, you'll secure the evidence, survey the scene, clear out junk and duplicates, expose hidden characters, unmask the aliases with a reference table, and rescue the dates. Every step uses built-in Excel tools and formulas. No experience beyond the basics is needed, and every formula is provided, ready to copy.

Late?Catch up in two minutes

The workshop runs in timed sprints and the whole room moves on together, so you can join at any sprint. Nobody needs to redo the earlier steps.

  1. Open the evidence and make your copy.

    Use the evidence link, then File → Create a Copy → Create a copy online. Work only in your copy.

  2. Check which sprint is on screen.

    It's in the slide title, e.g. "Sprint 3: Hidden Characters".

  3. Open the matching catch-up tab.

    The grey tabs at the bottom of the workbook show the data as it should look at the start of each sprint. Use the table to pick yours, and work in that tab from then on.

  4. Grab the formulas from the Formulas tab.

    Click the green cell, click in the formula bar, select all and copy, press Esc, then paste into the first empty cell under your new column header. In a catch-up tab, change Working to that tab's name in the counter formulas.
